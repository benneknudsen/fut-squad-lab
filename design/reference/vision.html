    03 · Options page

    A preferences screen for a technical user: every control shows its real value in tabular figures. Sliders are live here so the ranges can be judged by feel.

    04 · Mark, lockup and icon set

    A FUT card with its top-right corner chamfered, holding a four-node formation: the card is what the product manipulates, the formation is what it builds, the chamfer is the corner the solver did not spend. Drawn on a 32-unit grid — card 24.8 units, chamfer a true 45°, 3.6 units of clear space built into every file. Nothing EA-owned: no badge, crest, club mark, league mark or player likeness is used anywhere. Below 20 px the master is replaced by a re-cut 16 px variant — outline 2.6, three nodes, heavier connectors — because the scaled-down master fuses into a blob. The app icon puts the mark on its own tile so it survives a light and a dark toolbar alike.

    05 · Token swatches

    tokens.css is the source of truth; this is its visual half. Three surface levels, one accent, three semantic colours and four card states. Contrast is measured against the surface each token is actually used on.

    06 · Keyboard, motion and states

    The panel is driven by a keyboard as well as a mouse: slot rows, lock toggles, alternatives and every control in the options page are reachable without a pointer, and focus never moves on a re-solve.
